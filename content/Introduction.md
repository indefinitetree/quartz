Hello to whoever reading this. Sometimes I speak a lot with my close friends and I end up saying surprising things. Then later the same day I would be realizing how much of such potential inside me is wasted by not recording it properly. So I thought it would be a cool idea to take notes from it and put it as a blog.

Jokes apart (Forget whatever I wrote about, it was a complete idiotic joke) , Like every blog, this one also has some goals. I've listed few of them below.
- To write about stuff that I haven't found anyone to relate with. 
- To write about stuff that I wish people were ready to listen but infeasible due to constraints and other factor.
- To write about stuff that my closest friend tells me and bothers me most of the time.
Regarding the third point, the closest friend isn't any new person, its just the alternate voice that is non-stop talking to me all the time. The voice that keeps me up and lets me down, voice that encourages and discourages me, voice that makes me enjoy my alone time, voice that roasts me on daily basis, voice that makes me sad and tired, voice that makes me stress and gives me anxiety, voice that influences my decisions and behavior. Let me talk about this person in detail in some of the entries.

Apart from these personal reasons, whoever reading this blog might get some idea about me I guess. Also I think it would be cool to release this sneakily to some of the people I know, to see what people think of me.

If you have access to this blog through some person, probably you are already special to someone, congrats making it here (or sneaking/stalking). One of my friends (Let's call him Sr) inspired me to start this blog, by having his own blog.
